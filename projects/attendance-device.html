The Challenge

Institute attendance was being recorded manually on paper registers every day. The system had no central record, so calculating a student's present percentage meant counting dozens of register pages by hand. Paper registers were also easy to mark attendance for someone who was not present, and there was no reliable proof of attendance to support exam eligibility. Compiling the monthly report consumed a large part of the office's administrative time.

My Approach

I selected and deployed two biometric attendance terminals, connected them to the attendance database over the campus LAN, and handled the full enrolment and reporting workflow — from mounting the hardware to producing the monthly attendance report.

01

Device Selection & Placement

Selected two ZKTeco terminals supporting fingerprint, face recognition, and RFID card verification. Placed one at the main entrance for students and a second at the office entrance for teachers and staff, keeping the two groups on separate devices.

02

Installation & Network Setup

Mounted both terminals, wired them into the campus LAN with Cat6, and assigned static IP addresses so the attendance server could reach each device reliably instead of depending on DHCP leases.

03

Enrolment & Data Sync

Enrolled 300+ students and 40+ teaching and support staff across fingerprint, face, and RFID methods, then connected both devices to the ZKTime/ZKBio attendance database so records pushed automatically.

04

Reporting Integration

Configured scheduled time synchronisation so device clocks never drifted, and set up the ZKTime reporting workflow so the monthly attendance report could be generated and exported instead of compiled by hand.

Key Highlights

👆

Multi-Modal Verification

Fingerprint, face recognition, and RFID card — so a damaged finger or a forgotten card does not stop someone being marked present.

🔀

Separate Student & Staff Devices

Two dedicated terminals keep the large student population and the smaller staff population on independent, clearly separated records.

🌐

Static IP on Campus LAN

Both terminals hold fixed addresses, so the attendance server connects to them reliably with no manual reconnection after a power cut.

📊

Automated Monthly Reports

Attendance data pushes to the database continuously, so the monthly report is generated rather than assembled by hand.

Maintenance Routine

Ongoing work to keep both terminals accurate and reporting reliably:

  • Biometric Enrolment — enrolling new students and staff, and re-enrolling when a template fails to read
  • Template Backup — backing up fingerprint and face templates after every enrolment batch so a device failure is recoverable
  • Time Synchronisation — monitoring clock drift and keeping both terminals on the correct network time
  • Log Export & Sync Troubleshooting — resolving failed pushes to the database and correcting wrong times in exported reports
  • Firmware Updates — applying device firmware updates and adding new users to the database
  • Sensor Cleaning — routine cleaning of the fingerprint sensor, camera lens, and RFID reader surface

Results

Attendance for 300+ students and 40+ staff is now captured automatically at the point of entry, with no paper registers in the process. Because every record is tied to a biometric or card identity, marking attendance for someone who is not present is no longer possible. Records are available centrally as they are captured rather than reconstructed at month end, which removed most of the manual work from the monthly attendance report.
