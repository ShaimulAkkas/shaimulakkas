📅 2024 👤 Security Infrastructure Lead 🏫 Campus-wide ✅ Completed
HD IP Cameras

The Challenge

The campus had no centralized surveillance system. Computer labs, classrooms, and administrative areas were unmonitored, creating security vulnerabilities. There was no recording infrastructure, no motion detection, and no way to review incidents after the fact.

My Approach

I planned the camera placement strategy, managed the cabling infrastructure, and configured the entire surveillance system from hardware mounting to centralized monitoring.

01

Site Survey & Planning

Surveyed all buildings, identified critical zones, planned camera angles and coverage areas for maximum visibility.

02

Camera Installation

Mounted HD IP cameras across labs, classrooms, and admin areas with strategic positioning for full coverage.

03

NVR & Storage Setup

Installed centralized NVR units, configured storage drives, set up motion-detection triggers and recording schedules.

04

Cabling & Monitoring

Managed Ethernet cable routing, connector termination, and configured centralized monitoring screens for security staff.

Results

The campus now has complete surveillance coverage across all critical areas. The system records 24/7 with motion-detection alerts, enabling rapid response to incidents. Centralized monitoring allows security staff to oversee the entire campus from a single station. The infrastructure supports future expansion with additional cameras and storage capacity.

Configuration Snippet

NVR motion detection schedule configuration:

nvr-motion-config.yaml 
# Motion Detection Configuration
motion_detection:
  enabled: true
  sensitivity: medium
  zones:
    - name: "computer-lab"
      cameras: [cam-01, cam-02, cam-03]
      schedule: "24/7"
      alerts: [email, push]
    - name: "classroom"
      cameras: [cam-04, cam-05]
      schedule: "08:00-18:00"
      alerts: [push]
    - name: "admin-area"
      cameras: [cam-06]
      schedule: "24/7"
      alerts: [email, push]

# Recording Schedule
recording:
  mode: "continuous"
  retention_days: 30
  resolution: "1080p"
  fps: 25
